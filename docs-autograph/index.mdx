# Meet Autograph

Autograph is a signature fieldtype for ExpressionEngine. Add an Autograph field to a channel and it becomes a signing pad: visitors draw their signature with a mouse, a finger or a stylus, and the signature is saved with the entry, or as an image in an upload directory.

It's made for [Channel Forms](https://docs.expressionengine.com/latest/channels/channel-form/overview.html). A Channel Form that includes an Autograph field shows the pad with no extra template work, and the signature is saved with the rest of the entry.

:::info These docs are for Autograph 2
Still on 1.x? Choose **1.x** in the version picker for its docs, and read the [upgrade guide](./prologue/upgrade-guide.md) before updating.
:::
