# Control panel

## Introduction

On the publish form, and on a member's profile, an Autograph field behaves one of two ways, chosen by **Sign in the Control Panel** in its [field settings](./field-settings.md). (In 1.x the setting was called **Save in Control Panel**.)

## Off: the signature, read-only (the default)

Editors see the signature as it was saved, with what's known about it: *Saved in the entry* or *Saved as signature_….png*, and when it was signed. There's no pad, so an editor can't sign on somebody's behalf.

- **Open** shows a file-saved signature in a new tab.
- **Remove** clears the signature when the entry is saved. Until then, **Keep** puts it back.

Saving the entry without touching the field keeps the signature exactly as it was. When there's no signature yet, the field says **Not signed yet.**

Use this when signatures must come from the person signing, through a [Channel Form](./channel-forms.mdx) or a [member form](./member-fields.mdx).

## On: the pad

The field shows the signing pad, with **Undo** and **Clear**, styled to match the control panel. A saved signature is drawn on the pad when the entry opens.

Editors can sign, sign over the saved signature, or **Clear** it, and the change is saved with the entry.

Use this when signatures are collected in person, on a tablet signed into the control panel.
