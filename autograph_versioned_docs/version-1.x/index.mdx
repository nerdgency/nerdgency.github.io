# Meet Autograph

Autograph is a signature fieldtype for ExpressionEngine. Add an Autograph field to a channel and it becomes a signing pad: visitors draw their signature with a mouse, a finger or a stylus, and the signature is saved with the entry as an image.

It's made for [Channel Forms](https://docs.expressionengine.com/latest/channels/channel-form/overview.html). A Channel Form that includes an Autograph field shows the pad with no extra template work, and the signature is saved with the rest of the entry.

:::info These docs cover Autograph 1.x
Autograph 2 is out, with signatures as files, a responsive pad, Grid and Fluid. Its [upgrade guide](/docs/autograph/prologue/upgrade-guide#from-1x-to-20) explains how a 1.x site moves across without changing its templates.
:::
