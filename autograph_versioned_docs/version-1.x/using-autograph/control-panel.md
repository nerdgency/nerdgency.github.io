# Control panel

## Introduction

On the publish form, an Autograph field behaves one of two ways, chosen by **Save in Control Panel** in its [field settings](./field-settings.md).

## Save in Control Panel off (the default)

The field is read-only. Editors see the saved signature as an image, or **Not signed yet.** when there isn't one. Saving the entry keeps the signature as it was.

Use this when signatures must come from the person signing, through a [Channel Form](./channel-forms.mdx), and never from an editor.

## Save in Control Panel on

The field shows the signing pad, with **Undo** and **Clear** buttons, styled to match the control panel. An existing signature is drawn on the pad when the entry opens.

Editors can sign, sign over the existing signature, or **Clear** it. The change is saved with the entry.

Use this when signatures are collected in person, on a tablet signed into the control panel, or when editors need to remove a signature.
