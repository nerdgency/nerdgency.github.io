# Formidable 1.0.0 release notes

Formidable 1.0.0 is the first public release of Formidable, a forms add-on for ExpressionEngine.

## Requirements

- ExpressionEngine 7.5 or later
- PHP 8.2 or later

See [Installation and updates](/docs/formidable/getting-started/installation).

## What is included

### Building forms

- A drag-and-drop **builder** with pages, sections and a field grid, every drag also available from the keyboard, and an **Overview** map of the form's conditions.
- **28 fieldtypes** in four groups: Inputs, Dates & Times, Options and Special. These include file uploads, a signature pad, Computed fields, ExpressionEngine's Captcha, Cloudflare Turnstile and hCaptcha. See [Fieldtypes](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/fieldtypes).
- **Conditions** that show, hide or require fields, skip pages and hide sections, evaluated identically in the browser and on the server. See [Conditions](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/conditions).
- **Built-in lists** for choice fields: countries, languages, U.S. states, Canadian provinces and territories, days, weeks and months.
- **Validation rules** from ExpressionEngine's own rule set, checked on the server.
- A **Field Library** of reusable fields that forms link to and override.
- **Limits and access**: a closing date, a maximum number of submissions, and members-only forms.
- **Capture**: build a form from existing hand-written markup with `{exp:formidable:capture}`.
- The **Formidable Form** channel field, so editors can put a form on an entry, including in Grid, Fluid, Bloqs and Pro Variables.

### Rendering

- One tag, `{exp:formidable:form}`, which renders a complete form from a theme, with every layer replaceable by your own markup.
- Three shipped **themes** (Default, Tailwind and Bootstrap 5) and an Email theme. Any of them can be copied into an ExpressionEngine template group and changed part by part.
- **Regions**: classes and attributes for every part of a form, from the control panel or the tag, layered from fieldtype group to field.
- Computed accessibility attributes: instructions and errors linked to their inputs with `aria-describedby`, and `aria-invalid` on fields with errors.

### Submissions

- A control panel listing with filters, chosen columns, **saved views**, statuses, editing and bulk deletion.
- **Exports** to CSV, JSON, XML and plain text, matching the current filter and columns.
- Per-field **encryption** at rest.
- `{exp:formidable:submissions}` to list submissions in templates, with ExpressionEngine pagination, and `submission_handle=` to let visitors edit their own.

### After a submission

- **Workflows** that send email, call a signed webhook, or push to a connection, each with its own conditions.
- **Connections** for Mailchimp, Campaign Monitor, Constant Contact, Dotdigital, HubSpot, Salesforce and Mailjet.
- A **workflow log** recording each run, what it sent and what came back, encrypted at rest, with **Run again**.
- **Dynamic recipients**: visitors choose who receives their message, without addresses reaching the page.

### For developers

- A **JSON API** for forms and submissions, with keyless access from your own pages and scoped, hashed API keys for everything else. See [JSON API](/docs/formidable/developer/json-api).
- Forms as JSON (`output="json"`) for headless front ends.
- **Extension points**: register fieldtypes and connection providers from your own add-on, and the `formidable_submission_complete` and `formidable_spam_caught` hooks.
- **Command line** tools for listing, capture status, themes, fieldtype sync and recalculating Computed answers.
- Seven **permissions** to grant roles separately.
- **Jump Menu** commands for every Formidable screen, plus picking a form to edit, view, export or check the workflow log of, and finding a submission by number.

### Security

Signed and encrypted form meta, CSRF protection on every submission (AJAX included), honeypots, a per-IP rate limit, duplicate detection, a return-URL allow list, and escaped output. See [Spam protection and security](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/spam-and-security).

## Known limitations

- File uploads go to upload destinations on your own server. Cloud-backed destinations are not supported for uploads.
- Multi-page forms step through pages in the browser and submit once. `page=` renders one page per request, as its own submission.
- A workflow email's **Reply-to** is a fixed address.
