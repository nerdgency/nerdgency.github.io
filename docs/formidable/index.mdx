# Formidable

Formidable is a forms add-on for ExpressionEngine 7.5 and later. You build a form in the control panel, or wrap a form you already wrote, and Formidable takes care of the rest: rendering, validation, spam protection, storage, notifications, and handing submissions on to other services.

## What it does

**Build forms.** A drag-and-drop builder lays fields out on a grid, in sections and across pages. It ships with <FieldtypeCount /> fieldtypes: text inputs, dates, choices, file uploads, signatures, calculated values, CAPTCHA, Cloudflare Turnstile and hCaptcha. Fields can show, hide or become required based on other answers. Reusable fields live in a [Field Library](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/forms-and-fields#the-field-library).

**Use your own markup.** A single tag renders a complete form from a [theme](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/themes). Every part of that output can be taken over one piece at a time, down to individual attributes. If you already have hand-written forms, [`{exp:formidable:capture}`](/docs/formidable/guides/capture-existing-forms) builds the form from them and leaves your markup untouched.

**Keep and review submissions.** Submissions are listed in the control panel with filters, saved views, custom statuses and exports to CSV, JSON, XML and plain text. Fields can be encrypted at rest. Templates can [list submissions](/docs/formidable/developer/tags/submissions) or let a visitor [edit their own](/docs/formidable/guides/edit-a-submission).

**Act on them.** [Workflows](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/workflows) send email, call webhooks and push to [connections](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/connections) such as Mailchimp, HubSpot or Salesforce, each with its own conditions. Every run is recorded in a log you can replay.

**Work headless.** A [JSON API](/docs/formidable/developer/json-api) serves forms and submissions and accepts new submissions, using the same validation as a posted form.
