# Meet Formidable

Formidable is a forms add-on for ExpressionEngine 7.5 and later. Build a form in the control panel, or keep the form you already wrote, and Formidable handles everything after: rendering, validation, spam protection, storage, notifications and handing submissions on to the services you use.

## Why Formidable?

Most form tools make you choose. Either a builder owns your markup, or you own your markup and write the plumbing yourself. Formidable doesn't make you pick.

- **One tag, a complete form.** `{exp:formidable:form form_handle="contact"}` renders a finished, accessible form from a theme. That's the whole template.
- **Then take over as much as you like.** Swap one class, one attribute, one field's markup, or the whole form. Every layer you don't replace keeps working.
- **Or bring your own.** Wrap a hand-written form in `{exp:formidable:capture}` and Formidable builds the form behind it, leaving your markup exactly as you wrote it.
- **The server is the source of truth.** Conditions, required fields, computed values and limits are decided again on submit, whatever the browser sent.

## Where to start

New to Formidable? Read these three in order. They take about fifteen minutes, and everything else will make sense afterwards.

1. [Installation](/docs/formidable/getting-started/installation): requirements, and the two folders to copy.
2. [Your first form](/docs/formidable/getting-started/your-first-form): build a contact form and put it on a page.
3. [How it works](/docs/formidable/getting-started/how-it-works): the pieces, and the path a submission takes through them.

Already comfortable? Jump to the [form tag reference](/docs/formidable/reference/tags/form), or learn to [render a form your way](/docs/formidable/displaying-forms/rendering).
