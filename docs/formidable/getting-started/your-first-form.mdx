# Your first form

Let's build a small contact form, put it on a page with a single tag, and follow a submission back into the control panel. It takes about five minutes. You'll need Formidable [installed](./installation.mdx).

## Create the form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new form">

Open **Add-Ons › Formidable**, then **Forms**, and choose **New**. Name it *Contact Us*. Formidable suggests the handle `contact_us`.

Templates find a form by its handle, so pick one you won't want to change.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add some fields">

Once the form is saved, its edit screen opens on the **builder**. Drag three fields from the **Add field** palette into the page:

- a **Text** field called *First Name*
- a **Text** field called *Last Name*
- an **Email** field called *Email Address*

Click a field to edit its label, instructions and validation. Each field gets a handle from its label (`first_name`, `email_address`). That's the name its answer is posted under, and the name you'll use for it in templates.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Email required">

Open *Email Address*, switch to its **Validation** tab and turn on **Required**. An Email field already checks that the answer is a real address.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Put it on a page

Add the form tag to any template:

```ee title="templates/default_site/contact.group/index.html"
{exp:formidable:form form_handle="contact_us"}
```

That's the whole template. With only a handle, the tag renders the complete form from your site's theme: the `<form>` element, its hidden processing inputs, every field, error messages and the submit button.

## Submit it

Visit the page, fill the form in and submit it. With no `return=` parameter, the visitor comes back to the same page and the form shows its success message.

Try it again with the email left empty. The page shows the error beside the field and keeps everything else you typed.

## Find the submission

Back in the control panel, the [dashboard](/docs/formidable/control-panel/dashboard) lists your new submission under **Latest submissions**. Open it to read the answers, change its status, or edit it. The form's **Submissions** screen lists every submission, with filters and exports.

## What the tag produced

Here's that one-line template, rendered by a real install. Its *Contact Us* form has the fields above and a few more, and the site uses the Tailwind theme. The excerpt shows the start of the form and its first field.

<CapturedExample
  name="minimal"
  title="The whole form, from one tag"
  start='<form id="contact_us"'
  end={'<input id="first_name" name="first_name" value="" type="text" aria-describedby="first_name-instructions" class="block w-full rounded-md border border-gray-300 px-4 py-2 shadow-sm">'}
/>

A few things worth noticing:

- **The form posts to a Formidable action** (`?ACT=…`). You never write that URL yourself.
- **A hidden block comes first.** It holds a signed `_formidable_meta` value telling the server which form this is and how it was rendered, ExpressionEngine's CSRF token, and a honeypot that bots tend to fill in.
- **The input's `id`, `name` and `type` come from the field.** Its instructions have an id the input points at with `aria-describedby`, so screen readers announce them.
- **The classes come from the theme**, and from attributes saved in the control panel. You can change them without touching this template. See [Regions and attributes](/docs/formidable/displaying-forms/regions-and-attributes).
