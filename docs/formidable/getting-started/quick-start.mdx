# Quick start

This walkthrough builds a small contact form, puts it on a page with one tag, and follows a submission into the control panel. It assumes Formidable is [installed](./installation.mdx).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the form">

Open **Add-Ons › Formidable**. The first screen lists your forms. Choose **New**, give the form a **Name** such as *Contact Us*, and check its **Handle**: `contact_us`. Templates find a form by its handle, so choose one you will not want to change.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fields">

Once the form is saved, its edit screen has the **builder**. Drag fields from the **Add field** palette into the page: for this walkthrough, a **Text** field called *First Name*, another called *Last Name*, and an **Email** field called *Email Address*. Click a field to edit its label, instructions, validation rules and attributes. Each field's handle (`first_name`, `email_address`) is the name its answer is posted under and the name templates use for it.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it on a page">

Add the form tag to any template. With only a form handle, the tag renders the complete form from the site's theme: the `<form>` element, hidden processing inputs, every field, error messages and the submit button.

```ee title="templates/default_site/contact.group/index.html"
{exp:formidable:form form_handle="contact_us"}
```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit it">

Visit the page, fill the form in and submit it. With no `return=` parameter, the visitor comes back to the same page and the form shows its success message. If a required field is empty, the page shows the errors and keeps what was typed.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the submission">

Back in the control panel, the form's row on the **Forms** listing shows its submission count. Open the form's **Submissions** to see the entry, change its status, or export it.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the tag produced

Here is that one-line template rendered by a real install. Its *Contact Us* form has the three fields above plus a few more, and the site uses the Tailwind theme. This excerpt shows the start of the form and the first field.

<CapturedExample
  name="minimal"
  title="The whole form from one tag"
  start='<form id="contact_us"'
  end={'<input id="first_name" name="first_name" value="" type="text" aria-describedby="first_name-instructions" class="block w-full rounded-md border border-gray-300 px-4 py-2 shadow-sm">'}
/>

A few things to notice:

- The `<form>` element posts to a Formidable action URL (`?ACT=…`). You never write that URL yourself.
- `{form_meta}` produced the hidden block: a signed `_formidable_meta` payload that tells the server which form this is and how it was rendered, the CSRF token, and a honeypot input that bots tend to fill in.
- The field's `id`, `name` and `type` come from its handle and fieldtype. Its instructions have an id the input points at with `aria-describedby`, so screen readers read them.
- The classes come from the theme and from attributes saved in the control panel. You can change them without touching this template. See [Regions and attributes](/docs/formidable/core-concepts/regions-and-attributes).
