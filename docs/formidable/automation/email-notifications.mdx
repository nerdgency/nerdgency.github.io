# Email notifications

## Introduction

Emails are sent by [workflows](/docs/formidable/automation/workflows). Each workflow sends one email. Add one per audience: one for your team, one for the visitor.

## Email your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a workflow">

Open the form, go to its **Workflows** tab and add a workflow. Name it (for example *Notify sales*), leave the event as **Submission Created**, and choose **Send an email**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recipients">

Add a recipient row for each address: a typed address, a member, or every member of a role. Each row is delivered as **To**, **Cc** or **Bcc**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the subject and choose the message">

Leave **From** blank to use the site's webmaster address, or set your own. **Reply-to** is optional and takes a fixed address. The subject can include answers with `{field:HANDLE}`:

```text title="Subject"
New enquiry from {field:first_name} {field:last_name}
```

For the message, choose the email theme's **Submission** message, which lists every answer in a table, and keep **Use the email layout** on.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">

Submit the form, then open **Formidable › Workflow Log**. The run shows who it went to, the subject and body as sent, and whether the mail server accepted it.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send the visitor a copy

Add a second workflow with an email action and a **Form Field** recipient pointing at the visitor's email field.

A form field can only receive a workflow email when it is safe for it to do so, so first open that field and turn on **Allow as Workflow Recipient** on its **Advanced** tab. This lets anyone who submits the form choose an address your site will email, which is what a "send me a copy" field is for. Turn it on only for fields like this one.

To make the copy optional, add a Checkbox field such as *Email me a copy*, and give the workflow a condition: **Email me a copy** *equals* the checkbox's checked value.

## Let visitors choose the recipient

Let visitors pick "Sales", "Support" or "Billing" without the addresses ever appearing on the page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a choice field with Recipients">

Add a **Select**, **Radio Buttons** or **Checkboxes** field. On its **Options** tab choose the **Recipients** source, and add a row per choice: a short **value** (`sales`), the **label** visitors see, and one or more **email** addresses, separated by commas.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Point a workflow at it">

In a workflow's email recipients, add a **Form Field** row and pick this field.

  </Step>
</Steps>

The page, the [form's JSON](/docs/formidable/headless/form-json) and anything else a visitor can see carry only the values and labels. The addresses stay in the field's settings and are read only when the workflow sends. A visitor cannot add an address. A posted value that is not one of the field's values fails validation, and only the addresses in the field's own rows can ever be reached.

## Customise the email

The **Submission** message and the layout come from the [email theme](/docs/formidable/displaying-forms/themes#email-themes). Copy the **Email** theme into a template group to change them, and choose that group as the **email theme** in Configuration or on the form. Add your own messages as more hidden templates in the group, such as `_welcome.html`, and each workflow can choose one:

```ee title="system/user/templates/default_site/forms_email.group/_welcome.html"
<p>Hi {field:first_name},</p>
<p>Thanks for getting in touch about {form_name}. Here is a copy of what you sent:</p>
{submission_answers}
```

## Attach files

- **Attach uploaded files** attaches the files uploaded with the submission.
- **Attachments** adds files from ExpressionEngine's file manager to every email the workflow sends, such as a price list.
