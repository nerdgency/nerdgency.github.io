# Forms

## Introduction

A form is a named set of fields, laid out in pages and sections, with its own settings. You'll find every form on **Formidable › Forms**, where **New** starts another.

Every form has a **name**, for you, and a **handle**, for templates: `{exp:formidable:form form_handle="contact_us"}`. Pick a handle you won't want to change, because every template that shows the form uses it.

## Pages, sections and fields

A form is built from three nested pieces:

```text
Form
└── Page        a step the visitor moves through (Back / Next / Submit)
    └── Section a titled group of fields, such as an address block
        └── Field
```

Every form has at least one page, and every page at least one section. A form with one page shows no step navigation, just a **Submit** button.

- **Pages** have a name, a description, a heading level (2 unless you change it) and their own button labels. Empty labels fall back to Next, Previous and Submit. See [Multi-page forms](./multi-page-forms.mdx).
- **Sections** have a name, a description and a heading level (3 unless you change it). A section can also [repeat](./repeating-sections.mdx).
- **Fields** sit on a grid inside their section, a quarter, half, three-quarters or the full width of it.

Pages and sections can have [conditions](./conditions.md) that skip a page or hide a section based on earlier answers.

:::note No button or group fields
There's no "submit button" field and no "group" field. Every page draws its own navigation, and sections do the grouping.
:::

## The builder

A form's edit screen shows the whole form at once: each page is a panel, each section a box inside it, and each field a tile on its section's grid.

### Adding fields

Drag a field from the **Add field** palette to where it should go, or click it to add it to the end of the section you're looking at. Each section also ends with its own **Add field** picker, which you can search from the keyboard.

To add a field next to another, hover any edge of the field and choose the **+** that appears. Left or top adds before it; right or bottom adds after.

### Arranging fields

- **Edit** a field by clicking its tile.
- **Move** it by dragging, between sections and across pages.
- **Resize** it by dragging its right edge.
- **Pages and sections** move by their headers. Click a name to open its settings. A page or section can only be deleted once it's empty, and a form always keeps one of each.
- **Hidden fields** have no place on the grid, so they're listed above the pages.

Every drag has a keyboard route too. A field's menu offers **Width**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Move to** any section on any page, and page and section menus have their own **Move** options.

### Overview

The **Builder | Overview** switch above the builder shows a map of the form: every page and section, its conditions written out as sentences, and lines linking each condition to the fields it reads. It's the quickest way to understand a form someone else built.

## Duplicating a form

**Duplicate**, in a form's menu on **Formidable › Forms** or at the top of its builder, makes a new form from it, with its settings, pages and sections, fields, statuses and workflows. Submissions, counts and the workflow log stay with the original. The copy opens in the builder.

- The copy is named "*Name* (copy)", with a handle ending in `_copy` (then `_copy_2`, and so on). Rename both before you use the copy in a template.
- Fields keep their handles, so conditions, recipients, connection mappings and `{field:HANDLE}` in emails work unchanged. Computed fields are pointed at the copy's own fields.
- Fields linked to the [Field Library](./fields.mdx#the-field-library) stay linked.
- A copy of a captured form is an ordinary builder form. The template's capture tag still drives only the original.

Duplicating needs the **Manage forms** permission.

## Limits and access

A form's **General** tab can close it automatically:

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept submissions until** | After this date, the form shows its closed message instead of its fields. |
| **Maximum submissions** | Once this many submissions are stored, the form closes. |
| **Members only** | Only logged-in members may fill it in, and guests see a message. Follows the site setting unless the form sets its own. |

Each has its own message. The form is checked again when a submission arrives, so a page cached before the form closed can't sneak one through. Templates can place the message themselves with `{if form_closed}`. See [the form tag](/docs/formidable/reference/tags/form#closed-forms).

## Statuses

Every submission has a status, such as *New*, *In progress* or *Done*. Define the site's statuses on **Configuration › Statuses**, and a form's own on its **Statuses** tab, each with a name and a colour. New submissions get the default status.

Change a submission's status from its page in the control panel, filter the listing by status, and select by status in templates with `status=` on [`{exp:formidable:submissions}`](/docs/formidable/reference/tags/submissions). A status change can also [run workflows](/docs/formidable/automation/workflows#when-a-status-changes).
