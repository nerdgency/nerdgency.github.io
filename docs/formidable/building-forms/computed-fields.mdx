# Computed fields

## Introduction

A **Computed** field (in the **Special** group) holds a value worked out from other fields on the same form: a full name, an order total, an age. It updates as the visitor types. The server works it out again from the submitted answers, whatever the browser sent, and stores it like any other answer, so it appears in the submission, exports, emails, conditions and `{exp:formidable:submissions}`.

## What it can calculate

The field's **Computation** tab offers four kinds:

| Calculate | Example | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text from other fields | `{first_name} {last_name}` | Ada Lovelace |
| A number | `{quantity} * {unit_price}` | 47.98, shown as $47.98 |
| The time between two dates | from `{birthday}` to Today, in years | 34 |
| A date moved | `{start_date}` plus 30 days | 2026-11-10 |

Fields are named by handle, in braces. The editor's **Depends on** list shows which fields the saved calculation reads.

### Text

Literal text and fields, in any order. An empty field takes one separator with it, so `{street}, {city}, {postcode}` with no city gives `1 High St, AB1 2CD`, never a stray comma. Several answers from Checkboxes are joined with `, `.

### Numbers

A formula of numbers, fields, `+ - * /`, parentheses and a trailing `%` (`15%` is `0.15`):

```text
({subtotal} + {shipping}) * 120%
```

- A field must hold a plain number: `1234.5` or `-3`, not `1,234` or `three`. Otherwise the result is empty.
- An empty field makes the result empty, or counts as 0 if you choose **count as 0**.
- Division by zero gives an empty result.
- `sum({field})`, `count({field})` and `average({field})` total a field in a [repeating section](/docs/formidable/building-forms/repeating-sections) over its rows. Empty rows are left out, and `count` counts the rows that have an answer. A field answered once counts as one row.
- Results are rounded half away from zero to the decimals you choose, and **displayed** with your choice of thousands separator, decimal point, and text before and after (`$`, ` days`). The **stored** answer is always the plain number, such as `1234.50`, so it compares and exports cleanly.

There is no `eval` anywhere: the formula is checked and compiled when you save it, and saving refuses an unknown field, a syntax error, or a loop through other computed fields, naming the loop.

### Dates

**The time between two dates** is counted in days, weeks, months, years, hours or minutes. Months and years count complete ones, as an age does. **Count both ends** adds one day (1 to 3 October is 3 days). **A date moved** adds or subtracts days, weeks, months or years, keeping the day of the month where it can (31 January plus a month is the last day of February).

The dates can be date fields, other computed fields, **Today** or **Now**. Today and Now are the site's local date and time, so a page rendered just before midnight agrees with itself.

## Showing it

A new Computed field is **hidden on the form**: worked out and stored, but not shown. Turn on **Show on the form** to display it, read-only, where it sits in the builder. It renders as an `<output>` that screen readers announce as it changes, beside a hidden input carrying the plain value.

## Using it

- **Conditions** can read it. A field, page or section can depend on a computed value, such as showing a guardian's name when **Age** is less than 18. It compares as what it produces: a number, a date or text.
- **Workflow emails** include it as `{field:HANDLE}`.
- **Other computed fields** can use it. Each is worked out after the fields it depends on, wherever they sit in the form.

## When the calculation changes

A stored answer is a record of what was true when the form was submitted. Changing the calculation doesn't rewrite old submissions on its own. Instead, the form shows a notice offering to **Recalculate** them, with a progress bar. You can also run **Recalculate computed answers** from [Utilities](/docs/formidable/control-panel/utilities), or, for a large form or a deploy script, use the command line:

```bash
php system/ee/eecli.php formidable:recalculate --form=order
php system/ee/eecli.php formidable:recalculate --all --dry-run
```

Recalculating does not change a submission's updated date or run workflows. Editing a submission in the control panel also works its computed answers out again.

Renaming a field a calculation uses changes nothing for it, because calculations refer to fields by id. The editor simply shows the new handle.
